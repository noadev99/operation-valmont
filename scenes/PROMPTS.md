# Images des scènes : comment les fabriquer

Génère les images avec un outil d'images IA (ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney…). Donne-lui à chaque fois
les deux photos de tes amis en référence, puis colle le prompt de la scène.
Format : 16:9 (paysage). Enregistre chaque image sous le nom indiqué, dans ce dossier `scenes/`
(.jpg, .webp ou .png, peu importe). Les images manquantes restent en dessin.

## Style commun (à coller au début de chaque prompt)
Cinematic semi-realistic digital painting, heist movie poster look, dramatic lighting, volumetric smoke,
film grain, shallow depth of field, wide 16:9 composition. Two robbers wearing red jumpsuits and
latex clown masks pushed up or half-removed (faces visible, based on the two reference photos:
one with dark swept-back hair and light stubble, one with long wavy light-brown hair, a backwards
black cap, a mustache and a goatee). Banknotes floating in the air. No text, no logo.

## Les 12 scènes (nom de fichier : situation)
- hack-action : in a dark server room, one robber typing fast on a laptop lit by green code, the other watching the door, tense.
- hack-ok : the wall of screens turns green, both robbers celebrate, fists raised, cameras displayed as looping.
- hack-ko : red alarm light floods the server room, screens flash INTRUSION, both robbers panic, one reaching to unplug.
- coffre-action : a giant bank vault door half-closed, one robber drilling the lock with sparks flying, the other holding a flashlight.
- coffre-ok : the huge vault door swings open, golden light and stacks of cash inside, both robbers grinning, duffel bags ready.
- coffre-ko : the drill snaps, smoke pours out, red alarm beacon flashing above the vault, both robbers panicking.
- garde-action : a dim marble corridor, both robbers crouched behind crates peeking at a distant security guard with a flashlight.
- garde-ok : the guard turns and walks away down the corridor, robbers exchange a relieved smile, thumbs up.
- garde-ko : the guard's flashlight beam hits them, tactical police in the background, robbers frozen with hands half-raised.
- fuite-action : night street, both robbers running toward a parked van with open doors, duffel bags, distant city lights.
- fuite-ok : inside the speeding van at dawn, bags of cash on the seats, both robbers laughing, golden sunrise ahead.
- fuite-ko : police cars with red and blue lights block the road, both robbers stepping out with hands up, SWAT silhouettes.
